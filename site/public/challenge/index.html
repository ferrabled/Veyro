What is Veyro Run?

An endless runner you play by moving: tilt your phone to steer and tap to jump, or choose Camera Mode (BETA) and move sideways to steer, hop to jump. It plays offline, there are no ads, and camera frames never leave your device — the privacy policy is the long version.
