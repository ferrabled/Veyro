Support

Veyro Run (com.ferrabled.veyro.run) · answered by the developer

Fastest way to reach us: ferrabled+veyro@gmail.com. It's a one-person studio — you'll get a reply from the person who wrote the code.

Does the camera record me?

No. Camera frames are processed in memory on your phone by a local model and immediately discarded — never recorded, never saved, never transmitted. Nothing the camera sees ever touches the network. The game does go online for cosmetics — checking which ones you own at launch, and buying or restoring one — and no camera data is involved in any of that. Details in the privacy policy.

Camera mode isn't detecting me well

Camera mode is in beta. What helps: prop the phone upright at roughly chest height 1–2 meters away, face the front camera, and make sure your face is reasonably lit. If it still misbehaves, tilt & touch mode is always available — and a report to the email above (phone model + what happened) genuinely helps fix it.

Does it work offline?

Yes — the whole game plays offline. The Daily Run is computed from the date on your device, with no server involved, so everyone gets the same track without the game going online. The network is used only for cosmetics: on launch the game asks our purchase provider which ones your device owns, and buying or restoring one needs a connection because Google Play handles it. With no connection nothing breaks — cosmetics just show as locked and every run still plays. What that check sends is in the privacy policy.

How do I join the closed test?

Email ferrabled+veyro@gmail.com with the subject "Veyro Run closed test" from the Google account you use on your Android phone, and you'll receive an opt-in link.

Purchases and refunds

The game now has optional cosmetic purchases: runner skins and a season pass. That is the whole shop — everything buyable is cosmetic, nothing gives a gameplay advantage, and the free download is the complete game. Payment is processed by Google Play under your own Google account; we never see your card details. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy; if something goes wrong, email us and we'll help sort it out.

I bought a skin and it's not there

Finish a run, tap Skins & store on the result screen, then tap Restore purchases — signed in to the same Google account you bought with — unlocks are tied to that account and come back on any device it's on. Restoring needs a connection. If it still doesn't appear, email us with your order number from your Google Play order history and we'll fix it.

Data deletion

Veyro Run has no accounts and no server-side profile, so there is no account to close. Your best scores and control-mode preference live on your device only and are removed when you uninstall the app.

If you have bought a cosmetic, that purchase is recorded by Google Play (in your own Google account's order history) and by RevenueCat, our purchase provider, so it can be restored later. Email ferrabled+veyro@gmail.com and we'll request deletion of the RevenueCat record — after that the purchase can no longer be restored to a new install. Full detail in the privacy policy.
