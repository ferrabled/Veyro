Camera mode & your privacy

Your face never leaves your phone

  • On-device onlyCamera frames are processed in memory by a local model and discarded. Never recorded, never saved, never transmitted.
  • Only if you choose itThe camera is used only in camera mode, only after you grant permission. Declining loses nothing — tilt & touch is the full game.
  • No accounts, no trackingNo ads, no profiles, no gameplay analytics. The game goes online only for the cosmetics shop — checking which ones you own, buying, restoring — nothing else you do leaves the device.

The Daily Run

One track. Everyone. Every day.

Each day the game builds one endless track from the date itself — the same track on every phone on Earth, with no server involved. Run it once or chase your best all day; at midnight UTC it's gone forever.

Ticket is illustrative — the real seed is computed inside the game, deterministically, and two devices produce byte-identical runs.
